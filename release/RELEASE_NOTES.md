# Ghar Hisaab 1.0.1 (گھر حساب)

Mukammal ghar ka hisaab kitaab aur ledger — 100% offline, mehfooz aur baghair kisi ads ya internet ke.

Android 5.0 (Lollipop) ya us se naya chahiye. Install karne ke liye APK par tap karein; Android ek baar 'unknown apps' ki ijazat maangega.

---
### Taza Tareen Tabdeeliyan (What's New in v1.0.1):
1. **Naya Naam (App Rename):**  
   App ka naam **"Ghar Hisaab" (گھر حساب)** rakh diya gaya hai taake ghar ka koi bhi fard (mard ya aurat) ba-asaani aur fakhar se ise istemaal kar sake.
2. **Bachat (Savings) Icon Update:**  
   Bachat ke sath mojood purana icon tabdeel kar ke halal aur clean coin symbol (`Icons.paid`) laga diya gaya hai.
3. **Mukammal Mehfooz (Data Preserved):**  
   Purana hisaab kitaab (entries, bachat, peshgi aur staff records) bilkul mehfooz rahega aur update ke baad khud-bakhud carry over ho jayega.
